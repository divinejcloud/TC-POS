Restaurant POS — Operations Guide
User Roles & Screens

Order Lifecycle
  1. PENDING COUNTER – order created, waiting at the Counter. Table: Waiting for Counter Confirmation.
  2. CONFIRMED BY COUNTER – paid via Pay & Confirm, sent to the kitchen. Table: Occupied.
  3. ACCEPTED BY KITCHEN → PREPARING – kitchen accepts and starts cooking with an ETA. Table: Preparing.
  4. READY – cooked; waiters/counter get an alert. Table: Ready to Serve.
  5. SERVED – delivered to the guest. Table: Needs Cleaning, then tap it to make it Available.
  6. CANCELLED – rejected at the Counter (only possible before payment) or merged into another order.
Dashboard Operations Workflow
  1. Create Order: tap + New Order, choose an Available table in the Basket (leave empty for Takeout), tap items, then Submit Order.
  2. Table cards: tap Available to reserve; Reserved to manage/clear; Needs Cleaning to mark clean.
  3. Serve / Transfer / Merge: manage active orders directly from the Dashboard list or grid.
Counter & Billing Workflow
  1. Pay & Confirm: apply Senior/PWD (VAT removed first, then discount applied, VAT-exempt) or manual discounts, select payment method or Delivery platform, and print receipt.
  2. Kitchen Ticket: print 80mm or A4 kitchen tickets at any time.
Kitchen Display System (KDS)
  1. Accept incoming confirmed tickets, enter prep minutes, start prep, and mark ready when cooked.
Admin Setting Operations
  • Configure restaurant name, VAT, service charge, Senior/PWD %, KDS mode, menu items, tables, categories, and staff accounts.
Reports & End of Day
  1. Generate Sales Reports or EOD Reports, print them, and close the POS for the day (downloads a JSON backup archive and resets daily orders/tables).
Offline & Troubleshooting
  • Offline changes queue locally in IndexedDB and sync automatically once reconnected.
