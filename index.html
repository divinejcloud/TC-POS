Restaurant POS Quick Guide

1. Waiter / Tables: Tap an available table to reserve it, or click New Order to submit orders to the counter.

2. Counter: Confirm and process payments (Cash, GCash, Maya, Card, Delivery, or Senior/PWD 20% VAT-exempt discounts).

3. Kitchen (KDS): Accept confirmed orders, set preparation minutes, and mark orders ready when plated.

4. Admin & Reports: Generate consolidated Sales Reports, run End-of-Day Z-Readings in Thermal or CSV format, and manage branches, menus, tables, and users.
